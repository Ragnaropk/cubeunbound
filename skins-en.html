How to wear it: unzip it and copy the race folder (for example human) into mods/skins, next to the game. On Android, copy it over USB to Android/data/com.cubeunbound.game/files/skins/ (or com.cubeunbound.premium); human skins also go in by tapping each .vox and choosing "Open with" Cube Unbound. Then play a character of that race. Everything is in the wiki guide.

Upload your skin

Choose the race and upload its MagicaVoxel pieces. You see how it looks before sending it. We review every skin before it goes up.

This race uses . Each file is named after its piece (head.vox, body.vox…; the _f ones are the female version). Pieces you leave out stay as in the game. Up to 48 KB per piece and 96 KB in total.

    We keep the .vox files, the name, the author and, if you add it, your contact (see the privacy notice).
