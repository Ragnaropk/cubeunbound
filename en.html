Cube Unbound

A voxel action RPG and sandbox. A 64 km world that gets more dangerous the farther you go, ten races, levels 1 to 500, dungeons full of hordes, mounts that fly and swim, and everything open to mods.

Windows, Android and Linux · No installer · Custom engine · Made by one person in Mexico · In English and Spanish

A world in rings

You start in the Cradle and danger grows with every kilometre: 40 biomes in six zones, caves with seven strata, walled cities and 16 dungeons with hordes of hundreds.

Your character, your way

Ten races with three skill branches each, gear with six rarities and a +0 to +20 upgrade you can see from afar, and a forge where you sculpt your weapons cube by cube.

Live, not just fight

Tame more than 30 mounts, fish, farm, cook, build your home with hundreds of furniture pieces and open your stall in the square.

With friends and with mods

LAN for up to 8 players or a dedicated server that stays on. All content is open files: creatures, items and recipes in JSON, models in MagicaVoxel.

A custom engine, small and fast

Cube Unbound runs on an engine written from scratch in C++20 with no third-party libraries. The whole game fits in a zip of a few megabytes, starts instantly and switches between DirectX 11 and OpenGL without closing.
